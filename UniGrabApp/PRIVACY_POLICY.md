# Privacy Policy for UniGrab

**Effective Date: June 12, 2026**

UniGrab is built as a fully open-source, client-side application. We respect your privacy and do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

### 1. No Data Collection
UniGrab does not collect any personal identification information (PII), device information, browsing history, or download history. 

### 2. Client-Side Execution
All search operations, media URL parsing, and file downloading are executed **locally on your device**. There is no third-party mediation server. 

### 3. Local Storage
Downloaded media files and terminal logs are stored locally on your device in your app-specific sandbox directory. They are never uploaded to any remote server.

### 4. Direct Connections
When you query or download media, the application establishes direct network connections to the target media hosting services (e.g. YouTube, SoundCloud, etc.) to fetch the streams. These requests are subject to the privacy policies of those respective platforms.

### 5. Open Source Audit
Since this application is fully open source, you are welcome to inspect the source code on GitHub to verify our data handling practices.
